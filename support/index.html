FAQ · COMMON REPAIRS
Which formats can remusic play?+
remusic plays your on-device library with native decoders powered by the mpv media engine — MP3, FLAC, AAC, Opus, WAV and more. Albums play gapless, so live records and DJ mixes keep their seams.
Does it work without internet?+
Yes. Everything in your local library — songs, playlists, favorites — plays fully offline. Network is only needed for streaming and downloads, and download quality is up to you.
How do I back up my library?+
remusic can save your playlists, favorites and listening history into a single backup file from the backup section of the app. Restore it on a new phone or after a reinstall and everything comes back — you never start over.
Where do my downloads go?+
Finished downloads appear in your Library under Downloads. The download manager shows progress in your notifications, and you can pause, resume or retry any transfer.
How do I slow a song down to learn it?+
Two tools: speed & pitch are independent — slow the track without dropping its key — and the A-B loop repeats any passage you mark. With synced lyrics in karaoke view, it's a full practice rig for singers and musicians.
Does music keep playing when I close the app?+
Yes — background playback keeps going when you leave or swipe the app away, with full lock-screen and notification controls, headphone and Bluetooth button support, and a slide-to-skip mini player. A sleep timer can fade your night out.
What are scenes?+
A scene is a preset that bundles equalizer, effects, speed, crossfade, volume and visualizer into one switch — Night, Car, Gym, Coding, Flight, Party, Sleep are built in, and you can make your own.
Is remusic really free?+
Yes — free, ad-light, no account, no paywall. Your files stay yours.
