What is ReMusic?

Why can't I hear an audio effect?

Some effects work best with headphones or earphones. Check your device volume, audio output and the effect controls in ReMusic.

Why are lyrics not showing?

Lyrics availability can depend on the track and the source of the music. If lyrics are unavailable for a particular song, try another track or check your connection.

Can I use ReMusic offline?

ReMusic supports offline listening for music that has been made available for offline use within the app, subject to the applicable source and content rights.
